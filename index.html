01 / THE MISSION

Curiosity turns
into capability.

Our mission is to provide a welcoming space to all who would like to learn about robot technology via hands-on project development for competitions. The club also strives to establish a community that supports STEAM students with current and future career goals.

We’re a student-run team building robots from the ground up. Members split across sub-teams and come together to ship competition-ready systems. New to robotics? Perfect. We’ll teach you.
